# Kindling

Kindling is a multi-addon suite for **Minecraft Bedrock Edition** that revamps the survival experience. It keeps the vanilla game you know and makes surviving in it matter more: your body gets tired, the weather and seasons push back, and resources have to be found rather than tripped over.

Each addon is its own behavior pack (and resource pack where needed). They work on their own, and they're designed to work better together.

| Addon | Behavior pack | Resource pack | What it changes |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Body](#body) | `Body/` | `BodyRP/` | Stamina, hunger, sleep, hidden stats, danger |
| [Climate](#climate) | `Climate/` | `ClimateRP/` | Heat, cold, seasons |
| [Ores](#ores) | `Ores/` | `OresRP/` | Ore generation, tin, unrefined drops |

---

## Body

The player module. You spawn as vanilla Steve; Body changes how your character's physical condition works.

- **Stamina.** A second bar sits above the XP bar. Every action costs stamina: sprinting, swimming, jumping, climbing, mining, attacking, blocking with a shield and drawing a bow drain it faster than it regenerates, while crouching and walking slowly recover it (on console, the cost scales with how far the stick is pushed). Placing blocks and tool work like tilling or stripping logs cost a little each. Wading through shallow water costs more than land but less than swimming. At zero stamina you crawl, can't mine or attack, and sink in water.
- **Hunger.** Hunger caps how much stamina you can regenerate. Above 75% food there's no limit; on an empty stomach up to 75% of the bar is locked (shown in black). Hunger doesn't drain hearts the vanilla way; hearts only regenerate when both food and stamina are full.
- **Insomnia.** Skipping nights weakens you: strength, speed, maximum stamina and regeneration drop (never to zero, and no worse after 10 days) until you sleep through a night. Phantoms are turned off.
- **Hidden stats.** Strength, agility, regeneration, vitality, and resistances to fire, heat, cold and poison.
- **Danger.** Killing villagers or animals raises your danger rating. Villagers and passive mobs keep their distance, flee at high danger, and wolves go on alert. Danger fades slowly, and sleep helps.

No new items, mobs or foods.

Tune it in `Body/scripts/config.js`. Test commands: `/kl:get`, `/kl:set <stat> <value>`, `/kl:add <stat> <value>`, `/kl:reset`, `/kl:help` (or `.k get` in chat).

## Climate

Makes the world's temperature something you have to deal with.

- **Heat and cold** by biome and time of day, with a midday heat spike and a midnight cold spike. The Nether is mildly hot.
- **Seasons.** A year of four seasons, 7 Minecraft days each:
  - **Spring:** mild; winter snow melts away.
  - **Summer:** more heat.
  - **Autumn:** falling leaves, colder rain.
  - **Winter:** more cold, snow building up in temperate and cold biomes.
- **Works with Body:** Body's heat and cold resistances apply, and insomnia makes nights colder.
- **HUD.** With Body installed, a colored label next to the stamina bar shows how hot or cold you are (Chilly, Cold, Freezing / Warm, Hot, Overheating). Without Body, Climate shows a short warning in the action bar.

Check the current state with `.kl climate` and `.kl season` in chat.

## Ores

Rewrites underground ore generation so mining feels like finding a real deposit. Affects **new chunks only**.

- **Rare multi-ore deposits.** Instead of many small single-ore blobs, there's about one deposit every 4 chunks. Each one is a long, thick vein (around 40 or 80 blocks) running in one direction, mixing a main ore with accessory ores:

  | Deposit | Y range |
  |---|---|
  | Iron + coal | -24 to 80 |
  | Coal seam + trace iron | 0 to 128 |
  | Copper + tin + trace gold | -16 to 96 |
  | Gold + redstone | -64 to 32 |
  | Lapis + trace diamond | -64 to 16 |
  | Diamond + lapis (rare) | -64 to -16 |

  Between deposits there are only rare single blocks and small pockets. Emerald stays in the mountains.
- **Tin ore**, in stone and deepslate, dropping raw tin.
- **Unrefined drops.** Diamond, emerald, lapis and redstone ores drop rough gems; stone drops rocks, deepslate drops rubble, and so on. Silk Touch still gives the ore block.
- **Surface outcrops.** Thin ore slabs on exposed stone hint at what's below.
- **River gold.** Gold-flecked gravel in rivers drops extra nuggets.
- **Nether tweaks.** More gold in the wastes, more quartz in the forests, a little extra debris in basalt deltas.

Deposits are generated by `tools/gen-veins.mjs`: edit its `CONFIG` block and run `node tools/gen-veins.mjs` to regenerate and validate them.

## Installing

**1. Get the packs into Minecraft**, either way:

- **Bundle (easiest):** run `node tools/build-mcaddon.mjs` and double-click the resulting `dist/Kindling.mcaddon`. Minecraft imports every pack.
- **By hand:** copy each behavior pack folder into `behavior_packs` and each resource pack folder into `resource_packs` under:

  ```
  %appdata%\Minecraft Bedrock\Users\Shared\games\com.mojang\
  ```

  For development, link the folders into `development_behavior_packs` / `development_resource_packs` instead, so edits show up without copying. Don't also import the bundle on the same PC, or Minecraft will have two copies of each pack.

**2. Set up a world:**

1. Create a new world (Ores only affects newly generated chunks).
2. Turn on the experiments **Beta APIs** and **Custom Biomes** (needed by Ores).
3. Add the behavior packs for the addons you want. Each one adds its resource pack automatically.
4. Play in Survival or Adventure mode.

Each pack folder has an `INSTALL.txt` with more detail.

## Planned addons

- **Recipes:** refining rough gems and raw tin, alloys like bronze and steel, and clothing that protects against heat and cold.
- **Enchants, potions and foods:** new enchantments for armor, weapons and tools, plus potions and foods that buff Body's stats.
- **Towns:** realistic villages, towns and cities on flat ground, connected by roads with signs.

## Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock 1.21 or newer
- Script API `@minecraft/server` 2.1.0
- Node.js (only for the scripts in `tools/`)

## Tools

| Script | What it does |
|---|---|
| `tools/build-mcaddon.mjs` | Bundles all packs into `dist/Kindling.mcaddon` |
| `tools/gen-veins.mjs` | Regenerates and validates the ore deposit features |
| `tools/gen-tin-textures.mjs` | Builds the tin textures from the vanilla iron ones |
| `tools/gen-hud.mjs` | Regenerates the stamina bar and climate label HUD (`BodyRP/ui/kindling_stamina.json`) |

Body and Climate share per-player values (insomnia, resistances, heat, cold) through `kl_*` scoreboard objectives, because each behavior pack's dynamic properties are private to it.
