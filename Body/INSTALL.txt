Kindling — Body
===============

This is the player module of **Kindling** (a survival suite, not a
"better Minecraft" overhaul). Later packs can sit beside it:
Beasts, Hearth, Conditions, and so on.

What Body does
--------------
Stamina, hunger lock, insomnia, hidden attributes, danger
(kills make villagers, animals, and wolves treat you as a threat).
No new items, mobs, foods, or tomes.

Install
-------
Minecraft for Windows stores packs here (not the old Packages folder).
Put the behavior pack in **behavior_packs** (not development_):

  %appdata%\Minecraft Bedrock\Users\<your-id>\games\com.mojang\behavior_packs\KindlingBody\

Put the resource pack (HUD) in **resource_packs**:

  %appdata%\Minecraft Bedrock\Users\<your-id>\games\com.mojang\resource_packs\KindlingBodyRP\

The resource pack is the `BodyRP` folder (manifest.json at its root).

2. Create or edit a world.
3. Experiments: enable **Beta APIs** if the pack asks for them.
4. World → Behavior packs → add **Kindling Body**.
5. World → Resource packs → add **Kindling Body**.
6. Survival / Adventure. Creative is ignored.
7. Re-enter the world. Chat should say "Kindling Body scripts running".

If a world already had "New Player" enabled, this is the same pack
(same UUID). You should not need to add it twice.

What you should feel
--------------------
- Spawn is vanilla Steve while full, fed, and rested.
- A second XP-style bar sits above the real XP bar (stamina). Hearts
  and hunger sit above that. Bright yellow = remaining, dark yellow =
  spent, black = hunger-locked.
- Crouching and normal walking recover stamina slowly (crouch more than
  walk). On console the cost scales with how far the stick is pushed.
  Walking always recovers, even with heavy insomnia.
- Wading shallow water (feet on the bottom) costs more than land, less than a swim.
- Sprint, swim, jump, mine, hit, climb, shield block, and bow draw drain
  faster than regen. Placing blocks and tool work (tilling, stripping
  logs, paths) cost a little each.
- Food above 75%: no lock. Empty food: at most 75% locked (25% left).
- Hearts do not drip from hunger. At 0 food, ½ heart per Minecraft day.
- Hearts regen only when the food bar is full and stamina is full.
- Skip nights or take insomnia: strength, speed, stamina max, and regen
  drop (never to 0). It stops getting worse after 10 sleepless days.
  Only a full night's sleep (waking in the morning) clears it. Lying
  down briefly or dying does not. Phantoms are turned off by this pack.
- Stamina 0: crawl, no mine/attack, sink in water.
- Danger rises when you kill (villagers more than animals). Villagers,
  traders, and passive mobs keep away (around danger 50). At 75+
  passives flee; at 100 they run hard. Hostiles (drowned, zombies)
  never flee from danger. Hold a mob's food to still lure it.
  Tamed mobs do not flee. Wolves go on alert at high danger.
  Danger fades slowly, and sleep helps.

Test commands
-------------
`!k` is a Minecraft cheat prefix, so it will not work. Use:

  /kl:get
  /kl:set danger 50
  /kl:add strength 10
  /kl:set fire 0.5
  /kl:reset
  /kl:help

Or chat (no slash):

  .k get
  .k set danger 50
  .k add agility 5
  .k reset

Stats: strength agility regen vitality fire heat cold poison danger stamina insomnia
Resists are 0 to 0.9. Danger is 0 to 100.

Tweaks
------
Edit `scripts/config.js` (costs, regen, lock %, etc.).
