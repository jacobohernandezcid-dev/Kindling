Kindling — Climate (seasons cycle)
==================================

Heat / cold by biome and time, plus a simple year of seasons.

Requires
--------
**Beta APIs**. Soft-works with **Kindling Body** (heat/cold resists + insomnia).

Seasons (7 Minecraft days each)
-------------------------------
- **Spring** — mild; winter snow melts in temperate biomes
- **Summer** — more heat; mild warmth outside deserts
- **Autumn** — leaf-fall particles; colder rain
- **Winter** — more cold; snow layers in temperate + cold biomes
  (not deserts). Temperate biomes get night chill.

Day and night keep vanilla length. Each season day starts at sunrise.

Also
----
- Midday heat spike / midnight cold spike
- **Insomnia** (Body): skipped nights make cold build and midnight spike worse;
  sleep clears Body insomnia and eases Climate nights again
- Nether: mild heat (unchanged idea)

Not included (engine limits)
----------------------------
Sun path, real leaf recolor, forcing snow weather in deserts.

Check
-----
  .kl climate
  .kl season

Install
-------
